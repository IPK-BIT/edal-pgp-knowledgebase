After the internal review is initiated, please wait while the IPK Data Stewardship Team is actively assessing your submission. If the reviewers identify any issues that prevent the immediate acceptance of your dataset, you will receive a **Revision Request** via email.

If you receive a request for changes, read the feedback carefully. The email will contain specific comments from the reviewers explaining what needs to be fixed. Once you have addressed all points, submit the dataset again. It will go back into the review queue.

:::tip[Pro Tip]
Responding quickly and thoroughly to revision requests significantly speeds up the time to publication and DOI assignment.
:::

Once your revision is accepted (or if no revision was needed), you will receive the acceptance email.
